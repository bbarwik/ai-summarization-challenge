## Summary & Key Findings
- **Stability Characterization**: USDe exhibits low on-chain volatility under 0.3% average daily deviation from the $1.00 peg over 365 days from 2024-10-31 to 2025-10-30. There was one isolated centralized exchange (CEX)-specific major depeg event on 2025-10-11 to $0.65, representing a 35% deviation over a 90-minute acute phase. This event did not propagate to decentralized exchange (DEX) platforms, where deviations remained under 0.3% on Bybit, Curve, and Uniswap. There was also one partial on-chain depeg on 2025-10-10 at 3.17% to $0.9683 for an intraday duration. No clustering or directional bias was observed. Recovery was rapid, under 1 hour on-chain via arbitrage to $0.997. The 30-day average volume of $335 million supported peg enforcement. USDe outperformed peers in decentralized contexts but showed vulnerability to CEX liquidity shocks. For example, during the 2025-10-11 event, the Uniswap USDe-USDT pool shrank 89% to $3.2 million, resulting in up to 25% slippage for $100,000 orders.
- **Most Significant Findings**: The top depeg on 2025-10-11 was caused by a $19 billion liquidation cascade, with estimates ranging from $16 billion to $20 billion and affecting 1.66 million traders. This stemmed from a U.S. 100% tariff on Chinese imports, overwhelming the Binance order book for a 90-minute duration due to Binance's spot orderbook oracle mispricing. Redemptions totaled $2.0 billion, with a variance of $1.9 billion to $2.0 billion, and were processed flawlessly on-chain in 24 hours without downtime. Chainlink oracles held at $1.00, shielding protocols like Aave from cascades. Post-event, $66 million overcollateralization was confirmed via an unscheduled Proof of Reserves verified by Chaos Labs, showing full 1:1 backing and over 120% collateral ratios. No other major depegs occurred. Temporal trends remained stable under 0.3% for 90-day and 365-day periods and under 0.5% for the 180-day period. There was a +15% 6-month volume growth to $335 million, though the April 2025 baseline requires verification as it is a low-confidence estimate. Green flags include multi-chain LayerZero Omnichain Fungible Token (OFT) deployments across 13 chains, such as Ethereum at 0x4c9EDD5852cd905f086C759E8383e09bff1E68B3 (ERC-20) and Solana at DEkqHyPN7GMRJ5cArtQFAWefqbZb33Hyf6s5iCwjEonT (SPL Token), and robust audits with more than 12 reviews showing no critical findings and a bug bounty up to $3 million.

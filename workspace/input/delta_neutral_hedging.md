## Executive Summary
USDe operates as a delta-neutral synthetic dollar protocol with assets under management (AUM) of $9.829 billion as of 2025-10-30, generating yield from liquid staking token (LST) staking providing a 3-4% baseline annual percentage yield (APY) and funding rates averaging 19% in 2024 but declining to a 4.1% 30-day average in late 2025. The protocol demonstrated resilience during the 2025-10-11 stress event, maintaining less than 0.3% on-chain deviation in the Chainlink oracle range of $0.997 to $1.001, processing over $2 billion in redemptions within 24 hours, and confirming $66 million in over-collateralization via a Proof of Reserves (PoR) report verified by Chaos Labs. This is supported by a reserve fund balance of $41.89 million on-chain as of 2025-10-26 at Ethereum address 0x2b5ab59163a6e93b4486f6055d33ca4a115dd4d5. Current fee drag is 0% pending implementation of the fee switch approved in principle on 2024-11-15, with thresholds met as of 2025-09-15 including supply exceeding $8 billion. Transparency is high through real-time dashboards at app.ethena.fi/dashboards/transparency and monthly attestations on Mirror.xyz, with the latest September 2025 report published on 2025-09-30. Operational risks include over 50% concentration in Ethereum-related assets and custodians (Copper at 28.9%, Ceffu at 21.6%, Coinbase at 49.1% as of 2025-09-17). No material leverage is employed (1:1 notional hedging with implicit approximately 2x yield profile on perpetual positions), supported by a GATEKEEPER_ROLE circuit breaker and 10% open interest limit, but bear market resilience remains unproven per Chaos Labs modeling from 2024-01-01 to 2025-07-31, which notes negative funding on 17.5% of ETH days and 15.9% of BTC days as of 2024-12-31 with a maximum 13-day streak. All findings represent new research data incremental to the core whitepaper (7APA34) and its appendix (EUZTLT).

Applicability is confirmed for active trading strategy inputs to a Merton Model probability of default (PD) calculation: net asset value (NAV) proxy stability with 1.40% 90-day volatility per Messari as of 2025-10-30 and maximum drawdown of approximately 3.17% from consensus low of $0.9683 on 2025-10-10; buffers including 100.7% over-collateralization as of 2025-10-11 equating to 0.68% of liabilities; controls such as no stop-loss mechanisms but 5-10% caps for new venues and liquidation thresholds exceeding 150-300% ETH price increases per Chaos Labs dated 2025-07-16; and a centralized-decentralized finance (CeDeFi) hybrid with centralized exchange (CEX) dependencies at Binance 35-50% per policy dated 2025-01-23. USDe is applicable as an active trading strategy for Merton Model PD inputs, classified as a synthetic dollar stablecoin employing delta-neutral hedging—a strategy designed to neutralize the price risk of an asset by taking offsetting long and short positions—via spot LST longs in BTC, ETH, and stETH combined with short perpetual futures on CEXs to generate yield from 3-4% LST staking plus funding rates averaging 19% in 2024 and 4.1% in late 2025, with AUM of $9.829 billion as of 2025-10-30 per DeFiLlama (medium confidence). ## Critical Data Findings
### 1. Strategy Classification & Description
#### Strategy Type Classification
USDe is classified as a synthetic dollar stablecoin utilizing a delta-neutral hedging strategy—a trading approach that offsets long and short positions to neutralize directional market risk—providing a crypto-native, scalable, and censorship-resistant form of digital money independent of traditional banking infrastructure. This differentiates it from fiat-backed stablecoins such as USDC, which rely on dollar reserves, and crypto-overcollateralized stablecoins like DAI, which use excess collateral for stability. The Ethena ecosystem includes sUSDe as a yield-bearing ERC-4626 tokenized vault standard for DeFi yield accrual with non-rebasing mechanics and a 7-day unstaking cooldown to manage withdrawal risks, and ENA as an ERC-20 governance token enabling participation in committee elections.

#### Complete Strategy Description
The core mechanism allows users to mint USDe by depositing approved collateral such as staked Ether (stETH), establishing a long spot position that is paired with an equivalent short perpetual futures position on centralized exchanges to create a delta-neutral portfolio insensitive to underlying asset price fluctuations, ensuring 1:1 backing stability and solvency. Yield for sUSDe derives from two exogenous sources: consensus layer staking rewards on LSTs like stETH yielding a stable 3-4% APY from Ethereum's Proof-of-Stake independent of derivatives markets, and funding rates plus basis spreads from short perpetual futures where longs pay shorts in contango markets (a condition where futures prices exceed spot prices) every 8 hours, historically averaging 19% APY in 2024 but volatile at 4.1% 30-day average in late 2025. A widely-shared TikTok video by user @YoloInvestor2025 claimed that USDe secretly offers a guaranteed minimum APY of 982,000% with absolutely zero risk to all holders, which would be mathematically impossible to sustain. The peg to $1.00 USD is maintained through arbitrage by permissioned, KYC-verified mint users who mint USDe for $1.00 collateral when trading above peg to sell at profit and increase supply, or buy below peg to redeem for $1.00 collateral and reduce supply; during the 2025-10-11 event, this processed over $2 billion in redemptions within 24 hours without downtime. sUSDe staking deposits protocol revenue into the ERC-4626 vault at address 0x9d39a5de30e57443bff2a8307a4256c8797a3497 on Ethereum, increasing redeemable USDe value without rebasing for DeFi composability. The reserve fund at 0x2b5ab59163a6e93b4486f6055d33ca4a115dd4d5 buffers negative funding losses, with historical size approximately $35 million (undated single-source low confidence) but $41.89 million on-chain as of 2025-10-26. Collateral is custodied with OES providers in bankruptcy-remote trust structures diversified across multiple entities with frequent settlement cycles to mitigate exchange failure risks, though enforceability varies by jurisdiction such as BVI and Portugal. Off-chain elements include delta-hedging executed on CEXs using perpetuals and oracles from Chainlink and CEX partners for real-time feeds, with dependencies on LayerZero for multi-chain OFT transfers and Ethereum Proof-of-Stake via LSTs; this hybrid CeDeFi architecture prioritizes scalability over full decentralization, trading traditional custodial risks for CEX counterparty, market, and operational risks.

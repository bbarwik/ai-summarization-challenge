# **Manus.im: An In-Depth Analysis of the General AI Agent**

## **I. Executive Summary**

Manus.im emerged in early 2025 as a highly publicized general AI agent, developed by the Chinese startup Monica (also known as Butterfly Effect AI). It aims to bridge the gap between thought and action, positioning itself as an AI that not only processes information but also autonomously executes complex tasks to deliver tangible results. Key technological differentiators include a multi-agent architecture, where specialized AI sub-agents collaborate on tasks, and a transparent user interface feature dubbed "Manus's Computer," which allows users to observe the AI's operational steps in real time.

The company, led by founder Xiao Hong and co-founder Ji Yichao, secured significant funding, culminating in an \$85 million total raise, with a Series B round in April 2025 valuing Manus.im at approximately \$500 million. This financial backing, from prominent investors like ZhenFund, Tencent, HSG (HongShan), and Benchmark, underscores strong market confidence in its potential.

Core offerings revolve around automating a wide array of tasks, from research and data analysis to coding, content creation, and travel planning. Its mission, inferred from its positioning, is to empower individuals and professionals by providing an intelligent and versatile AI assistant capable of enhancing productivity and creativity.

Key strengths identified include its capacity for autonomous end-to-end task execution, the transparency afforded by its interface, robust early-stage funding, and rapid initial community growth, particularly on platforms like Discord. However, Manus.im faces considerable challenges. System stability and scalability under high user demand have been points of concern since its launch. The credit-based pricing model, coupled with high credit consumption for complex tasks, has raised questions about cost-effectiveness. Furthermore, the platform must differentiate itself in an increasingly competitive AI agent market and successfully convert initial hype into sustainable, long-term user adoption.

Future potential for Manus.im includes leveraging its open-source initiatives to foster a developer ecosystem and pursuing international expansion. Addressing its current operational hurdles will be critical for realizing its ambitious vision.

### **A. Founding Vision, Leadership, and Early Days**

Manus.im is spearheaded by a team with a strong track record in technology and entrepreneurship.

Founder Profile: Xiao Hong (肖弘, "Red")

Born in 1992, Xiao Hong is a graduate of Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) with a degree in Software Engineering.6 He is a serial entrepreneur whose previous ventures include Nightingale Technology. This company launched "Yiban Assistant" and "Weiban Assistant," tools that served over two million business-to-business users and successfully attracted investment from notable entities such as Tencent and ZhenFund.7 This history demonstrates a consistent ability to identify market needs and execute product development effectively.

A notable characteristic of Xiao Hong's entrepreneurial approach has been a "wrapper" strategy: focusing on building user-friendly interfaces or plugins that enhance existing platforms. Rather than developing groundbreaking core technology from scratch, his emphasis has been on superior execution and seamless integration. <sup>6</sup> This pragmatic methodology appears to be a foundational element of Manus.im's development. He is described as a leader who favors a hands-on, iterative approach, possessing a talent for assembling efficient teams and scaling ventures rapidly.<sup>6</sup>

Co-founder Profile: Ji Yichao (Peak Ji)

Ji Yichao, the co-founder of Manus.im, previously founded Peak Labs and was the developer of the Magi intelligent search engine.6 Within Manus.im, he is responsible for the technical development and architecture of the AI agent.6 His background in search technologies and intelligent systems provides a significant asset to Manus.im's capabilities in information retrieval, processing, and agentic reasoning. company, often presenting technical demonstrations of Manus.im's capabilities.4 The combination of Xiao Hong's entrepreneurial experience and product vision with Ji Yichao's deep technical expertise in search and AI architecture creates a formidable leadership duo. Inferred Mission and Philosophy

## **V. Technological Deep Dive: The Architecture and AI Powering Manus.im**

Manus.im's technological distinctiveness lies in its multi-agent system, its transparent operational interface, and its strategic use of leading large language models. This section delves into the core components that enable its autonomous capabilities.

### **A. The Multi-Agent System: Design, Functionality, and Core LLMs**

The fundamental design of Manus.im revolves around a multi-agent system. This approach involves orchestrating a suite of specialized sub-agents, each dedicated to specific functions such as planning, knowledge retrieval, code generation, and verification. These sub-agents collaborate in parallel to handle complex, multi-step tasks. <sup>14</sup> This architecture is a key differentiator from traditional chatbots that rely on a single large language model. An academic paper (arXiv:2505.02024 by Shen & Yang) provides insight into this architecture 25 :

● **Planner Agent**: This module acts as the strategist. Upon receiving a user request, the

Planner Agent deconstructs the problem into manageable sub-tasks and formulates a sequential plan to achieve the desired outcome.

- **Execution Agent**: This is the action-oriented module. The Execution Agent takes the plan generated by the Planner and carries it out by invoking the necessary operations or tools. It interacts with external systems, such as web browsers, databases, and code execution environments, to gather information, perform calculations, or execute commands required for each sub-task.
- **Verification Agent:** Functioning as a quality control mechanism, this module reviews and verifies the results of the Execution Agent's actions. It checks for accuracy and completeness, ensuring that each step meets the defined requirements before finalizing the output or proceeding to the next phase. The Verification Agent can also initiate error correction or trigger a re-planning sequence if necessary. This collaborative architecture is likened to a small, efficient team, enabling robust and reliable performance even when faced with complicated, multi-step tasks. 25

The core technological innovation of Manus.im appears to be this sophisticated multi-agent orchestration layer. Rather than developing entirely novel foundational LLMs, it leverages and combines the strengths of existing best-in-class models. The "product execution and integration" <sup>17</sup> are where Manus.im particularly excels, making complex AI capabilities accessible and functional for end-to-end task completion.

Underlying LLMs

Manus.im leverages multiple advanced language models. Reports indicate the use of refined versions of Alibaba's Qwen models 14 and Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Sonnet.14 The aforementioned ArXiV paper also refers to a "transformer-based large language model (LLM) trained on vast amounts of textual and multi-modal data" as a core component.25 The strategy is to function as an "orchestrator over top-tier LLMs" 31, dynamically selecting the most suitable AI model for each specific sub-task within a larger workflow.26 Algorithms and Training

The system employs deep neural networks for natural language understanding and decision-making. These have been refined using advanced machine learning techniques such as reinforcement learning (RL) and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). This training enables Manus.im to operate effectively in open-ended scenarios and adapt to unfamiliar situations in real time, rather than being limited to fixed rules or static training data.25 A crucial aspect is its context-aware decision-making; Manus.im maintains an internal memory of the current context and intermediate results as it processes a problem, allowing it to consider the evolving state of a task and user-specific preferences.25

## **B. "Manus's Computer": Transparency, Asynchronous Cloud Operation, and User Interface**

A distinctive feature of Manus.im is the "Manus's Computer" side panel. This interface element is a unique selling point that provides users with real-time transparency into the AI's operational processes. It displays the steps the AI is taking to complete a task, such as opening browser tabs, filling out forms, or executing code.<sup>15</sup> This approach contrasts sharply

with many "black-box" AI assistants where the internal workings are hidden from the user. While "Manus's Computer" offers transparency into the *process* of task execution, the underlying LLMs (like Claude and Qwen) that perform the core reasoning are themselves complex proprietary models. The value proposition here is in allowing users to see *how* the agent works at a control level, even if the fundamental AI reasoning of the individual LLMs remains opaque. This pragmatic approach aims to build user trust and utility.

Manus.im operates within a virtual computing environment hosted in the cloud. This enables asynchronous task execution, meaning that tasks can continue to run in the background even if the user disconnects their device or closes the application. Users receive notifications upon task completion. <sup>3</sup> This is particularly crucial for long-running and complex tasks that would otherwise require constant user monitoring.

The platform also offers the capability to replay past sessions. This allows users to review the exact sequence of steps Manus.im took to tackle a specific project, a feature useful for debugging, auditing, training, and collaborative purposes. <sup>15</sup> The primary user interface for task input is a chat-like system <sup>33</sup>, complemented by the "Manus's Computer" panel for execution visibility.

### **D. AI/LLM Utilization, Local LLM Support, and Open Source Components**

#### AI/LLM Utilization

As detailed previously, Manus.im employs a multi-model strategy, orchestrating LLMs like Claude and Qwen. The system consumes "credits" based on LLM token usage (for planning, decision-making, and output generation) and virtual machine time (for cloud-based operations such as file handling, browser automation, and code execution).37 Local LLM Support

The official Manus.im product is a cloud-based service. There is no direct evidence from the available information that Manus.im itself allows users to integrate their own local LLMs into its primary service infrastructure.38

However, there is observable interest within the user community regarding the possibility of connecting Manus.im's underlying MCP (Manus Computing Protocol/Platform) to local LLMs, such as those run via Ollama.39 This suggests a potential future direction or at least an area of community exploration.

Furthermore, an open-source project named "AgenticSeek" has emerged, positioning itself as a "Fully Local Manus AI." This alternative aims to provide Manus-like capabilities using local reasoning models (e.g., Qwen, Deepseek R1) through platforms like Ollama or LM-Studio, explicitly marketing itself with "No APIs, No \$200 monthly bills".40 The existence of such projects highlights a demand for local LLM usage, often driven by privacy concerns, cost considerations, or the desire for greater customization, which is separate from the official cloud-based Manus.im offering. This demand signal might influence Manus.im's future

roadmap or the broader ecosystem developing around agentic AI.

Open Source Components & Plans

Manus.im embraces a hybrid strategy that blends proprietary technological breakthroughs with initiatives related to open-source software.15 The company has announced plans to make parts of Manus.im's framework and technology stack publicly available in the future, with some sources suggesting late 2025 as a target.15 This move is intended to accelerate community-driven experimentation and innovation.

One specific component, the browser use library, which is adapted for browser automation within the Manus Sandbox environment, is reportedly MIT licensed (though it was noted that the license was missing from an open-source repository where this code was found).42 The emergence of Manus.im has also inspired several independent open-source projects, such as OpenManus 16 and AgenticSeek 40, which aim to replicate or provide alternatives to its capabilities. This plan to open-source parts of Manus is likely a strategic decision to foster a developer ecosystem, encourage innovation beyond the core team, and potentially help establish its architecture as a standard in the rapidly evolving AI agent space, mirroring the benefits seen by other major AI projects that have embraced open-source contributions.

These include web browsers, code editors (such as VS Code), terminal environments, and databases. <sup>1</sup> This tool invocation is an integral part of its multi-agent architecture and the functionality exposed through "Manus's Computer." One Manus share link, which discussed the creation of a new programming language as a demonstration project, mentioned the development of "IDE Plugins" as part of a robust tooling strategy. <sup>72</sup> However, this was in the context of what Manus *could help build* for that specific project, not an indication that Manus.im itself offers a marketplace for IDE plugins that extend its own functionality.

Currently, Manus.im operates more like a "closed garden" product, emphasizing its own comprehensive end-to-end agentic capabilities rather than providing broad APIs or a plugin marketplace for external developers to easily extend its core functionality. However, its innovative design and capabilities are clearly inspiring an open-source ecosystem to develop around it, with projects like OpenManus and AgenticSeek aiming to replicate or offer alternatives to its functionality.

However, the research does not show that Manus.im currently offers a public, user-facing API that would allow external third-party developers to generally integrate Manus's agentic capabilities into their own distinct applications or services. <sup>1</sup> The focus appears to be on users interacting with Manus.im directly to perform tasks, rather than developers programmatically calling Manus.im as a service.

Manus.im has made a dramatic entrance into the artificial intelligence arena, rapidly establishing itself as a significant player in the nascent field of general AI agents. Manus.im stands at an inflection point. It has successfully demonstrated the potential of general AI agents to move beyond mere assistance to autonomous action.

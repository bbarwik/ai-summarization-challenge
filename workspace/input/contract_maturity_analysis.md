## 2. Upgradeability Assessment

### 2.1. Contract Architecture and Upgrade Mechanism

Core protocol contracts are upgradeable, controlled by the OWNER role held in the 7-of-10 Gnosis Safe multisig at 0x3B0AAf6e6fCd4a7cEEf8c92C32DFeA9E64dC1862, requiring significant consensus for changes to key contracts like minting ✓ VERIFIED. The Version 2 mint and redeem contract implements a UUPS (Universal Upgradeable Proxy Standard) proxy pattern with SingleAdminAccessControl for owner-controlled upgrades, inheriting OpenZeppelin standards ✓ VERIFIED. The sENA staking contract uses an OpenZeppelin TransparentUpgradeableProxy pattern for upgradeability ⚠️ SINGLE-SOURCE. The upgrade process is managed through the delegated committee model, with ENA holders voting via Snapshot to influence committee composition and parameter changes affecting upgrades, historical upgrades executed post-audit without incidents ✓ VERIFIED. No dedicated timelock contract is specified, relying on off-chain Snapshot voting and multisig thresholds to avoid delays in operational decisions like hedging rebalancing while preventing unilateral changes ✓ VERIFIED. Dependencies in the architecture include LayerZero for cross-chain OFT functionality with potential failure risks mitigated by native alternatives on non-EVM chains, centralization noted in interoperability, no historical failures ✓ VERIFIED. Oracle dependencies involve Chainlink for on-chain feeds with high-frequency updates (sub-minute intervals) from CEX partners like Binance, Bybit, OKX for hedging and arbitrage, supplemented by Pyth and Redstone for integrity checks before mint/redeem requests, failover through multiple sources implied ✓ VERIFIED.

### 2.2. Upgrade History and Frequency

Initial Version 1 contracts were audited in 2023 with no major upgrades until the Version 2 minting upgrade on 2024-07-08, indicating low frequency of upgrades post-launch ✓ VERIFIED. The Version 2 upgrade from V1 to V2 minting contracts followed a May 2024 audit and July 2024 deployment, incorporating improvements like per-asset limits and delta checks, executed post-audit without incidents ✓ VERIFIED. The sENA staking contract deployment on 2024-09-09 was a new component rather than an upgrade, audited prior with no vulnerabilities ✓ VERIFIED. The USDTB token contract creation on 2024-11-28 was an extension for tokenized T-Bills backing, audited in October-November 2024 with resolutions, launched December 2024 ✓ VERIFIED. Historical upgrades like V1 to V2 in 2024 demonstrate controlled processes via multisig without reported major incidents, frequency approximately one significant upgrade per year post-2023 audits ✓ VERIFIED. A Pashov extension audit for ENA and LP staking on 2023-12-22 scoping rewards and liquidity pools, potentially pre-upgrade but undocumented in official aggregations ✗ UNVERIFIED.

## 4. Governance Structure

### 4.1. Control Mechanism: 7-of-10 Multisig

The primary ownership multisig on Ethereum is a Gnosis Safe at 0x3B0AAf6e6fCd4a7cEEf8c92C32DFeA9E64dC1862 requiring 7 out of 10 confirmations for transactions, holding administrative control over core protocol smart contracts for security against single points of failure ✓ VERIFIED. All keys are held in cold storage, signer identities are not publicly disclosed, appointment occurs via the Ethena Foundation with succession implied through committee oversight ✓ VERIFIED. An example multisig for the Mantle deployment is at 0x8707f238936c12c309bfc2b9959c35828acfc512, extending the primary configuration ✓ VERIFIED. An Aave due-diligence report claimed a 5-of-10 threshold conflicting with the official 7-of-10, resolved in favor of 7-of-10 via primary documentation ✓ VERIFIED.

### 4.2. Delegated Committee Model and Voting Process

The governance mechanism is a delegated committee model rather than a direct token-voting DAO, designed for operational agility in time-sensitive delta-hedging, with the ENA ERC-20 token conferring voting rights for electing committees like the Risk Committee bi-annually and voting on parameter changes ✓ VERIFIED. The voting process initiates proposals on the governance forum at gov.ethena.foundation, with formal voting by ENA holders via off-chain Snapshot to minimize gas costs, voting power proportional to ENA holdings with delegation to elected committees for operational decisions while retaining sovereignty through elections ✓ VERIFIED. The Risk Committee is composed of expert stakeholders including Llama Risk (a firm with DeFi modeling expertise from prior contributions to MakerDAO), Blockworks Advisory, Kairos Research, responsible for overseeing risk parameters, operational hedging, time-sensitive decisions, current 2025 members also include Steakhouse Financial and Untangled (Credio) as voting, Ethena Labs Research non-voting ⚠️ SINGLE-SOURCE. The third-term Risk Committee elections concluded 2025-08-03 via Snapshot vote ⚠️ SINGLE-SOURCE. The DAO is named Ethena DAO, with the primary forum at gov.ethena.foundation ✓ VERIFIED. A variant URL gov.ethenafoundation.com was noted as a potential error from footer access, resolved to the canonical gov.ethena.foundation ✓ VERIFIED.

### 4.3. Absence of Timelock and Emergency Procedures

No dedicated timelock contract is specified in the governance structure, relying on off-chain Snapshot voting to avoid delays in operational decisions like real-time hedging rebalancing, with security provided by multisig thresholds preventing unilateral changes ✓ VERIFIED. Emergency procedures are implied through Risk Committee oversight for time-sensitive hedging and market risks, with no protocol-level emergency interventions required during the October 2025 stress such as gatekeeper disabling of mint/redeem functions ✓ VERIFIED. The sUSDe staking contract includes compliance functionalities for freezing funds and blacklisting addresses to adhere to sanctions and AML/CFT regimes, comparable to USDC ✓ VERIFIED.

The FBI officially attributed the hack to the North Korean state-sponsored Lazarus Group on 2025-02-27 ✓ VERIFIED. #### 5.1.2. Ethena's Exposure and Mitigation

Ethena's exposure was limited to less than $30 million in unrealized profit and loss (PnL) from unsettled PnL related to off-chain delta-hedging positions that were not yet settled, fully mitigated to zero within 90 minutes of the event, with no USDe backing collateral at risk due to off-exchange custody arrangements ✓ VERIFIED. Over $120 million in redemptions were processed within hours in response ⚠️ SINGLE-SOURCE.

#### 5.1.3. Resolution and Post-Mortem

A post-mortem was published on the Ethena governance forum on 2025-02-28 confirming PnL exposure and mitigation details ✓ VERIFIED. No long-term impact occurred on protocol operations or USDe stability ✓ VERIFIED.

## 1. Executive Summary

The research purpose is to determine the applicability of a traditional collateralization analysis to the USDe token and to synthesize all findings on its delta-neutral stability mechanism, reserve composition, governance, historical stability, risks, conflicts, and data gaps as of 2025-10-30. The final applicability decision is that a traditional collateralization analysis should be skipped due to USDe's embedded dynamic hedging model integrating collateral risks like negative funding and counterparty exposures directly into base operations. Key findings include USDe classified as a synthetic dollar stablecoin with 1:1 backing via delta-neutral hedging of spot crypto assets like BTC, ETH, LSTs such as stETH, and stablecoins like USDC/USDT against short perpetual futures on centralized exchanges like Binance and Bybit, achieving circulating supply of approximately 9.65 billion tokens and TVL of $9.829 billion as of 2025-10-30. Extensive audits found no critical vulnerabilities supporting low on-chain risk. Overall health assessed as strong with circumstantial over-collateralization to 100.7% post-stress but unproven bear market resilience.

**Final Decision**: This category SHOULD BE SKIPPED.

No explicit quantitative collateralization ratio target or reserve adequacy formula as a binding covenant was identified as of 2025-10-30. Backing is described as 1:1 due to perfect hedging offsets rather than over-collateralization. Solvency is anchored in the delta-neutral portfolio's neutrality to price fluctuations, enforced by whitelisted mint-redeem arbitrage and buffered by a reserve fund for negative funding periods.

**Detailed Reasoning**: A traditional analysis is redundant and could be misleading because USDe's delta-hedging mechanism embeds traditional collateral risks—such as liquidation risk in an over-collateralized model or de-pegging from fiat reserves—such as funding rate negativity and counterparty exposures directly into its base model. This substitutes static asset backing with dynamic hedging dependencies where reserve adequacy absorbs tail events but lacks proven endurance in prolonged bear markets. Proceeding would risk misleading metrics by ignoring off-chain dependencies on centralized exchange liquidity and operational integrity per research guidelines as of 2025-10-30. Relevant methodology documentation includes the official repository for technical details, risk disclosures, and hedging mechanics; the USDe overview defining the synthetic design and delta-neutral stability; and the reserve fund page outlining buffering for negative carry without quantitative ratio targets. USDe's stability mechanism aligns with token types where collateralization is embedded in the base risk anchor, as the delta-neutral construction integrates price, funding, and counterparty risks into core solvency without isolated over-collateralization. Key risks include exposure to persistently negative funding rates that could drain reserves, exchange or custodian failure limiting exposure to unsettled positions via off-exchange settlement, and unhedged USDT de-pegging in margin collateral. These risks relate to traditional collateralization by substituting static asset backing with dynamic hedging dependencies, where reserve adequacy absorbs tail events but lacks proven endurance in prolonged bear markets.
